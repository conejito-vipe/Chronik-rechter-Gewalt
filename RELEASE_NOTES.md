## 🇺🇸 English

### v0.3.0 (26 Nov 2026)
**New**
- Interactive map `docs/index.html` with municipality colouring, state and municipality borders, filters, comparison table and individual cases.
- Statistics per municipality: population, men/women, Germans/foreigners, employment status, vocational qualification (census 2022), 2025 federal election.
- List of fatalities of right-wing violence (247 entries, Amadeu Antonio Stiftung) with own colouring and filter.
- Protected CSV `daten/chronik_public.csv` (streets masked, coordinates per municipality).
- Data sources, description texts and to-do list.

**Changed**
- 3,307 cases (previously 3,321). Further duplicates removed via text similarity, log `daten/dubletten_entfernt_log.csv`.

**Known**
- Licence open. Unemployed, crime, older elections and development since 2006 are missing. See `TODO.md`.
